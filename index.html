Beyond the Fatality Count

Who Is Hospitalised on Australia's Roads, 2011–2021

Road crashes are a leading cause of injury-related hospitalisation in Australia. While fatalities capture public attention, non-fatal injuries represent a continuing burden on individuals, families, and healthcare systems—accounting for 39,505 hospitalisations in 2021 alone.

05 / QUESTION 3: DISPROPORTIONATE IMPACT

First Nations & Remote Area Trajectories

First Nations road hospitalisations grew at six times the relative rate of non-Indigenous Australians, exposing severe geographic and demographic disparities.

First Nations Growth

+90.2%

Hospitalisations surged from 1,167 to 2,220, vastly outpacing baseline population growth.

Non-Indigenous Growth

+14.9%

Climbed from 31,997 to 36,755, mirroring general population expansion.

06 / CRITICAL LIMITATIONS

Data Quality & Caveats

01

Inclusion Breaks

Victoria (2012) and NSW (2017) excluded ED-only care, creating artificial downward trend steps.

02

2021 Mortality Field

All 2021 died-in-care rows report 0. Analysis is strictly constrained to 2011–2020.
